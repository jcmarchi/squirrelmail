# Deferral Register

Deferrals prevent silent technical debt. Use this file whenever a Buildline item is not implemented even though it may apply later.

| Foundation | Item | Decision | Reason | Risk | Activation Trigger | Review Point | Owner | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| _example_ | _example_ | deferred | _why not now_ | low/medium/high | _what makes it required_ | _when reviewed_ | _role_ | _current safety evidence_ |

Invalid deferral examples:

- "Later" with no trigger.
- "Not needed" with no relation to project profile.
- Security/ACL/data/testing omissions for implemented protected behavior.
